Arduino IDE (offline): student guide

Write, check and upload Arduino programs on your Chromebook. Nothing to install, and no internet needed.

1Open the IDE

  • In Google Classroom, open arduino-ide.html and download it. It won't run inside the Google Drive preview.
  • Open the Files app → Downloads and click arduino-ide.html. It opens in Chrome.
  • Tip: move it to My files so it's easy to find next time.

2Write your sketch

  • Type your code in the editor. Your work is saved automatically in this browser.
  • Examples has ready-made sketches, including one for every module in the sensor kit. Look for the HW‑number printed on your board (for example HW486_Photoresistor).
  • Libraries → Include adds a library's #include line for you.

3Check and upload

  • Plug the Arduino in with the USB cable.
  • Click ✓ (Verify) to check your code. Mistakes show in red: click one to jump to that line.
  • Click ➔ (Upload). The first time, pick Arduino Uno in the list Chrome shows and click Connect.
  • Wait for Done uploading.

4See what your board says

  • Serial Monitor tab → Connect. Choose the same speed as Serial.begin(), usually 9600 baud.
  • Serial Plotter tab draws numbers as a live graph. Name each line like this:
    Serial.print("light:"); Serial.println(value);

5Save your work to Google Drive

  • Autosave only keeps your work on this Chromebook. To keep it for real, click Save As and choose your Google Drive folder.
  • After that, Save updates the same file. A sketch with several tabs is saved as a folder.
  • To continue later, click Open and pick your file from Google Drive.

?If something goes wrong

  • The Arduino isn't in Chrome's list: try another USB cable (some only charge) or another USB port.
  • “Couldn't open the port”: click Disconnect in the Serial Monitor and close any other tab using the board.
  • Upload failed: unplug the board, plug it back in, and try again.
  • Nothing appears in the Serial Monitor: make sure the baud rate matches Serial.begin().
  • Red error you don't understand: look at the line it points to, and the line just above it (often a missing ;).

Shortcuts: Ctrl+R verify · Ctrl+U upload · Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+Shift+M Serial Monitor · Ctrl+Shift+L Serial Plotter
